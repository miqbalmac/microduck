Using your duck safely

Moves make a real robot walk, turn and move. Before teaching or playing a move, put your duck on a flat floor with space around it, away from edges, stairs, water and anything fragile, and keep an eye on it. Move previews show the move in a simulator; your duck may move a little differently. Follow the safety guidance that came with your Microduck. You use moves on your duck at your own risk.
