Before you write

  • My duck didn't learn a move. Check your duck is switched on, near your phone and connected to Wi-Fi with internet, then try again. You're only charged a credit once your duck confirms it learned the move.
  • Restore a subscription. In the app, open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also manage subscriptions here.
  • Refunds. Purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • A move doesn't work as shown. On the move's page, tap ⋯ then Report, or email us the move's name.

When you email, it helps to tell us your iPhone model, the app version (Settings → About) and what happened.
