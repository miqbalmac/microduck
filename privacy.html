Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

MicroDuck ("the app", "we", "us") lets Microduck robot owners browse moves and teach them to their duck. We built it to need as little information about you as possible. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

What we collect

  • An anonymous app ID. On first launch the app creates a random ID and keeps it on your iPhone. It isn't your name, email, phone number or Apple ID, and it isn't the advertising identifier. We use it to keep your credit balance, the moves you've unlocked and your subscription status. It stays on your iPhone if you delete and reinstall the app, so free credits are given only once per iPhone.
  • Credits and moves. Linked to that anonymous ID, our server keeps your credit balance, a history of credits added and spent, and which moves you've unlocked.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives the purchase information Apple provides (product, dates, renewal status) linked to the anonymous ID, so we can confirm Pro.
  • Reports you send. If you report a move, we receive the move, the reason you choose, any text you write, the app version and the platform.
  • Emails you send. If you email support, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your duck's name, settings, liked moves, recent searches and app preferences.
  • The Wi-Fi network name and password you enter during setup are sent over Bluetooth directly to your duck, so it can download moves. They are not sent to us.
  • Your duck's pairing PIN is used only between your phone and your duck.
  • Reminder notifications are scheduled on your iPhone; we don't use push notifications.
  • Move videos and pictures are downloaded and kept in a cache on your iPhone.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find, pair with and control your Microduck. We don't use Bluetooth to locate you.

Hugging Face

When you teach a move to your duck, your duck downloads the move's files from the Hugging Face Hub over its own internet connection. Hugging Face receives that request from your duck's network, as for any download. No personal information is sent from the app.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our server and database (United States).
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions.
  • Apple: processes purchases through the App Store.

They process data only to provide their service to us. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep credit and purchase records while the app is in use, and as needed for accounting and to resolve disputes. Reports are kept while they are being reviewed and then for our records. You can ask us to delete your records at any time (see below).

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes the data stored in the app on your iPhone.
  • To ask us to delete or show you the records linked to your app ID, email us. Because there are no accounts, we may ask you for details from the app to find your records.
  • You can turn Bluetooth and notifications on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases on a child's device are controlled by Apple's Family Sharing and Screen Time settings.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and its date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
